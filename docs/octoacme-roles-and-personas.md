# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Lead / Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads translate the product plan into working team execution. They align engineering capacity, dependencies, and sprint health with the broader project objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity, priorities, and delivery sequencing
- Coordinate technical dependencies across teams and systems
- Remove execution blockers and escalate cross-team issues
- Support sprint planning, risk tracking, and milestone forecasting
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to keep delivery realistic and aligned to goals

### Goals
- Maintain predictable, sustainable delivery
- Improve team clarity and execution quality
- Reduce friction between strategy, planning, and implementation

### Typical Communication
- Team standups, sprint planning, and dependency reviews
- Delivery status updates with PMs and stakeholders
- Technical trade-off discussions with developers and product leads

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to turn backlog items into executable work
- Collaborates with Product Managers on priority trade-offs and delivery feasibility
- Keeps Project Managers informed on schedule health, risks, and dependency changes

---

## Quality Lead / QA Manager

### Role Summary
Quality Leads own the quality strategy for the project, ensuring that user-facing and operational outcomes meet the expected bar before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, quality gates, and release checks
- Validate that features meet acceptance criteria and user value goals
- Partner with developers on defect triage and prevention
- Coordinate regression, smoke, and end-to-end validation efforts
- Support go/no-go decisions for releases and milestones

### Goals
- Improve product confidence and reduce defects in production
- Ensure quality is built into delivery, not treated as a final checkpoint
- Align testing work with business risk and release impact

### Typical Communication
- QA planning sessions and release-readiness reviews
- Defect triage with engineering and product partners
- Stakeholder reporting on quality risks and test coverage

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to define testable acceptance criteria and defect ownership
- Supports Product Managers in assessing whether outcomes are ready for release
- Provides Project Managers with quality risk signals needed for milestone decisions

---

## Security & Compliance Partner

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners ensure that projects meet required controls, privacy expectations, and risk standards without slowing delivery unnecessarily.

### Responsibilities
- Review security and compliance implications of planned features and integrations
- Identify and track security risks, control gaps, and remediation steps
- Support threat modeling, data handling reviews, and access control checks
- Coordinate with legal, operations, or governance stakeholders when required
- Help ensure release readiness aligns with policy and audit expectations

### Goals
- Reduce organizational risk and security exposure
- Create a repeatable path for secure delivery
- Balance governance requirements with product velocity

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and risk assessments
- Compliance checkpoints during planning and release readiness
- Escalation paths for urgent risk or policy exceptions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure implementation patterns and review requirements
- Works with Product Managers and Project Managers on risk tolerance and release gating
- Provides key input to stakeholders and sponsors when major security or compliance decisions are needed

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Liaisons connect product delivery teams with real customer experience, support issues, and operational realities.

### Responsibilities
- Surface customer pain points, support trends, and feedback loops
- Help translate support issues into actionable product or process improvements
- Coordinate with product and delivery teams on user impact and prioritization
- Support readiness for launches, adoption, and follow-up communication
- Identify recurring operational risks that require project attention

### Goals
- Improve customer value and satisfaction
- Ensure customer feedback is incorporated into product and delivery decisions
- Reduce operational friction between product launch and day-to-day usage

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback reviews and issue triage
- Cross-functional meetings on launch readiness and adoption health
- Support escalations and service-impact summaries

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds Product Managers with customer evidence and unmet needs
- Helps Developers understand actual user friction and operational constraints
- Provides Project Managers with customer-facing risks and communication needs

---

## Business Sponsor / Stakeholder Champion

### Role Summary
Business Sponsors and Stakeholder Champions provide strategic alignment, funding support, and decision-making authority when project priorities, dependencies, or escalations require executive attention.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic business value and project priority
- Sponsor resource alignment and cross-functional support
- Help resolve escalated decisions, trade-offs, and policy conflicts
- Validate that outcomes remain aligned with organizational goals
- Support communication with broader stakeholders and leadership

### Goals
- Keep the initiative aligned to business outcomes
- Enable timely decisions and accountability across departments
- Protect delivery momentum when trade-offs or escalations occur

### Typical Communication
- Executive check-ins, milestone reviews, and steering discussions
- Priority and trade-off decisions during planning or escalation
- High-level project updates and business outcome reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provides direction to Product Managers and Project Managers on business priorities
- Supports Delivery Leads and Quality Leads when cross-team dependencies or resource issues require sponsorship
- Helps align Customer Success, Support, and operational teams with project goals

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- The expanded set of roles helps clarify decision ownership, communication paths, and accountability across the project lifecycle.

---

## Role Interaction Snapshot
- Product Managers define intent and outcome.
- Project Managers coordinate plan, risk, and communication.
- Delivery Leads guide execution and team capacity.
- Developers implement and validate the solution.
- Quality Leads protect release quality and readiness.
- Security & Compliance Partners assess risk and policy alignment.
- Customer Success / Support Liaisons bring customer impact into project decisions.
- Business Sponsors provide strategic direction and support when decisions affect broader organizational goals.
