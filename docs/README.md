# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository contains the core project management guidance used by OctoAcme. OctoAcme follows a lightweight, cross-functional project model focused on clear ownership, iterative delivery, risk management, and stakeholder communication. The goal is to keep teams aligned from the earliest idea through planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement while ensuring that product value, quality, and delivery discipline remain visible to everyone involved.

OctoAcme’s process begins with initiation: teams validate the business problem, align stakeholders, define measurable success criteria, and decide whether the initiative should move forward. Once approved, planning turns the concept into a prioritized backlog, milestone map, and release plan, with dependencies, resources, and acceptance criteria clarified up front. During execution, the team uses daily standups, weekly delivery syncs, and milestone review meetings to manage progress, resolve blockers, and keep work moving toward defined outcomes. After release, the team captures lessons learned through retrospectives and turns improvements into action items so the process becomes more effective over time.

The operating model depends on a clear set of roles and responsibilities. Product leaders define outcomes, prioritize the roadmap, and measure impact; project managers coordinate schedules, risks, communication, and dependencies; developers build, test, and deliver software; QA validates readiness against acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input, sponsorship, and approvals. This clarity is reinforced through structured communication routines, including standups, weekly PM/PdM syncs, stakeholder updates, and escalation paths for blockers or critical issues. Risk and incident communication are handled with explicit templates and ownership so teams can respond quickly and keep stakeholders informed without confusion.

Quality and assurance are integrated into the process rather than treated as a final step. The team expects clear Definition of Done, test coverage for new logic, relevant integration and smoke tests, CI validation, security checks, and manual QA when needed. Release and deployment guidance emphasize controlled rollout, smoke testing, rollback planning, and post-deploy verification so changes are safe and observable. By combining strong roles, regular communication, disciplined execution, and continuous improvement, OctoAcme creates a repeatable project management approach that supports delivery at scale while remaining practical for cross-functional teams.

## Project management process overview

- Initiation: validate the business need, align stakeholders, define success metrics, and approve a go/no-go decision.
- Planning: create backlog priorities, estimates, milestones, dependencies, and a release plan.
- Execution: run standups, review progress, manage blockers, and maintain delivery momentum.
- Release & deployment: verify readiness, deploy safely, and document rollout and rollback steps.
- Retrospective & improvement: capture learning, assign action items, and improve the way the team works.
